Link Transit privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Link Transit helps you find and ride minibuses in Lusaka. You don't need an account and we never ask for your name, phone number or email. This page explains what the app uses, what leaves your phone, and why.

Your location never leaves your phone.
No account, no name, number or email.
Fare reports are sent only when you choose to.
No ads. We never sell your data.

Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your phone's location to:

  • show bus stops and routes near you,
  • plan trips from where you are, and
  • count down the stops during a guided ride, so it can tell you when to get off.

Your location stays on your phone. Link Transit does not send it to us or to anyone else. Location is only used while the app is open. You can say no, or turn it off at any time in your phone's settings. Then the app works from your saved Home or from town, and during a ride you tap through the stops yourself.

What is sent from your phone

WhatWhenWhy
Fare reports: the route, the fare you paid, whether you rode the whole route, the stops if you were on a guided ride, the time, the app version and a random ID made by the app when it was installed Only when you report a fare or answer "Did you pay…?" after a ride To keep fares up to date for everyone. When several riders report the same fare, it replaces the old one in the app. The random ID only lets us count different phones and stop one phone sending too many reports. It is not linked to your name, number or Google account.
Crash reports: what went wrong in the app, your phone model, Android version, app version and an ID for this install of the app When the app crashes or hits an error To find and fix bugs. Collected by Google Firebase Crashlytics.
Map requests: which part of the map you are looking at, and your internet address (IP), which every website or app connection includes When the map loads To draw the map. The map images come from our map provider, the OpenStreetMap Foundation (tile.openstreetmap.org).
Transit data downloads When the app updates its routes, stops and fares The app downloads the bus network so it can work without signal. Nothing about you is sent beyond the normal connection details.

What stays on your phone

Your saved routes, your Home, your settings, the list of updates you sent, and a copy of the bus network for when you have no signal. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of it.

Where data is kept and for how long

  • Fare reports are stored in our database, hosted by Supabase in the United Kingdom (London). We keep them for up to 2 years to understand how fares change, then delete them.
  • Crash reports are kept by Google Firebase for 90 days.

We don't sell data. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't share it with anyone except the service providers named here, who run the app for us.

Where the bus data comes from

The routes, stops and the first fares come from the Digital Transport for Africa (DT4A) survey of Lusaka. Riders' reports keep the fares up to date. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Your choices and rights

You can use Link Transit without ever reporting a fare or turning on location. Under Zambia's Data Protection Act, 2021, you can ask us what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Fare reports are tied only to a random install ID, so tell us roughly when you reported and on which route, and we will find and remove them. See how to delete your data.

Children

Link Transit is for the general public. It is not aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect anything about them.

Changes

If the app starts collecting something new, for example sharing a bus's live position with other riders, we will update this page before that feature goes live. The app will ask you first.
