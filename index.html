Guided rides

Fares that stay current

Works without signal

No account

How a guided ride works

Three steps, whether it's your daily route or your first time in that part of town.

Board

The app shows you which stop to walk to and which bus to look for.

Ride

It tells you how many stops to ride and counts them down as you go.

Get off

You get a heads-up before your stop, and the fare you should expect to pay.

Your privacy, plainly

We built Link Transit to need as little from you as possible.

Location stays on your phone

It's used to find stops near you and count down your ride. It is never sent to us.

No ads, no selling

We don't show ads, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Fare reports are anonymous

Reports carry a random install ID, not your name, number or Google account.
